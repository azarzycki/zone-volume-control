# Zone Volume Control – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Zone Volume Control is an Android app that controls the volume, inputs and zones of a compatible AV receiver on your home Wi-Fi network. It is developed and published by Andy Zarzycki as an independent project.

## The short version

The app collects no personal data, sends nothing to us or to anyone else, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. It talks only to the receiver you point it at, on your own network.

## What the app does with data

- **Receiver address.** The app finds your receiver on the local network and stores its IP address on your phone so it can reconnect faster next time. This address never leaves your phone.
- **Settings.** The app stores your volume-linking preference on your phone.
- **Network traffic.** The app sends commands to, and reads status from, the receiver over your local Wi-Fi network, using the receiver's own control interface. To find the receiver it also sends standard local-network discovery (mDNS) queries and, if needed, probes addresses on your Wi-Fi subnet. None of this traffic goes to the internet.

The app does not collect, store or transmit names, email addresses, location, contacts, identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or any other personal information.

## Permissions

The app requests these Android permissions, all for the local-network communication described above:

- Internet and network state: to communicate with the receiver on your Wi-Fi.
- Wi-Fi state and multicast: to discover the receiver with mDNS.

No permission gives the app access to your personal data.

## Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries. It does not share any data with Sony or with any other company.

## Children

The app is a utility for controlling audio equipment and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.
